<p class="lede">One person, and a lot of sky.</p>

<p>Some photographs are about a face. These are about scale: a single figure, small against a sky that's about to change its mind. A storm rolling in over the water, and someone who hasn't gone in yet.</p>

<figure class="narrow" style="--ar:0.9805"><img src="/photography/big-skies/big-skies-01.jpg" width="1506" height="1536" alt="Man in yellow shorts and a straw hat flying three kites on a white sand beach as dark storm clouds roll in" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>He has three kites up and a storm coming straight at him, and he isn't reeling them in.</p>

<p>An ongoing series, added as I go.</p>
