<p class="lede">Beer City 7s, July 2023. A full day of sevens rugby in Asheville under a bright summer sun.</p>

<p>Sevens is rugby with the space opened up: seven a side instead of fifteen, short halves, and nowhere to hide. Every player has to tackle, carry and run. I spent the day on the touchline chasing the same thing I chase at training, the ball arriving and the face of whoever is about to carry it.</p>
