<p class="lede">An evening training session with Asheville Rugby Football Club, ahead of the Beer City 7s.</p>

<p>No crowd, no scoreboard. Just a club side running drills on a summer evening in Asheville. What I chase at training is the half-second before contact: the ball in the air, hands up, eyes locked on it. In that moment everything else on the field disappears.</p>

<p>Rugby rewards watching the hands, not the ball carrier. A good pass is already gone by the time you see it, so I frame the receiver and wait for the ball to arrive in the shot.</p>
