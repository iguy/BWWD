<p>I find AI Design to be something so interesting. And especially passive AI design - I find to be the most interesting. </p>

<p>My Pixel 6 (while on the wireless charging stand) starts a photo slideshow of my recent photos. Often cropping the image or stacking one photo on top of the other and cropping both to squares. <a href="https://www.instagram.com/wmheninger/"  rel="noreferrer noopener">Wendy H.,</a> was the first person to speak to me be about duo images - and bring together two images to work together. </p>

<p>After hearing this image duo idea - I started to notice that my smartphone (while in charging mode) will create these awesome duo-images on my phone. I thought many of the images were so visually interesting that I started to make screen captures of the images. </p>
