<p>In June 2025 I went to Chicago for a men's wellness program. It was my first one. The week had hot yoga, CrossFit, a night of singing in a bar, dinner at a chicken house and a sharing circle. I sat out the hot yoga and the CrossFit, left the bar early, don't eat chicken, and opted out of the circle.</p>

<p>Looking back, that should have been the sign. I signed up for a program and skipped most of it. What I didn't skip was the city.</p>

<h2>Chicago on my own</h2>

<p>The Loop is built in layers: terracotta, carved stone, steel and glass. Walking it without a group, at my own pace, was some of the best time I spent there. Inside the Chicago Cultural Center is the Tiffany glass dome, and every surface around it is mosaic, glass or carving.</p>

<h2>From the river</h2>

<p>The architecture boat tour was the highlight of the trip, and I'd recommend it to anyone visiting Chicago. Shooting from a moving boat, you don't choose your angle. You look up, wait for the pattern to line up, and take the shot.</p>

<h2>Would I do it again?</h2>

<p>Maybe not. Seeing a city with a group of strangers was interesting, but the parts I enjoyed most were the buildings, the river, and the hours I spent in Chicago on my own.</p>
