<p class="lede">Twelve photographs, cut down from everything on this site.</p>

<p>What ties them together is attention. Every frame is someone, or something, so locked onto what's in front of them that the camera stops existing: a tackle at full commitment, a ball about to arrive, two people mid-conversation, a goat mid-leap. Most of them come from communities I belong to: a rugby club, a farm, a drag stage, the people around Asheville.</p>
