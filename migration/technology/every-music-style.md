<h3 class="has-large-font-size wp-block-heading">Your Music Encyclopedia</h3>

You can click on any genre to learn more about it, listen to samples, and explore related genres.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you are looking for a specific genre, you can use the search bar at the top of the page. <h4 class="has-tertiary-color has-text-color has-background wp-block-heading" style="background-color:#111111"><strong>Chicago House Music: </strong><br>A Legacy of Great Masters </h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To test <strong>Everynoise.com </strong>out, I searched for my personal favorite Chicago House Music. If you're a lover of House Music, then you've probably heard about the Chicago House Music scene. Chicago House Music is a genre that originated in Chicago, Illinois, in the early 1980s. The genre is characterized by its use of drum machines, synthesized basslines, and soulful vocals.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Chicago House Music was pioneered by a group of DJs and producers who were experimenting with new sounds and styles. These pioneers included Larry Heard, Frankie Knuckles, Marshall Jefferson, and many others. They created a new sound that was influenced by disco, funk, and soul music.</p>
