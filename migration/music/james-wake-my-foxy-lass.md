<div class="et_pb_text_1 et_pb_text et_pb_bg_layout_light et_pb_module et_flex_module"><div class="et_pb_text_inner"><h2>THE FOXY LASS | JAMES WAKE</h2>
<h3>James Joyce: Unlocked. Unedited. Unstoppable.</h3>
<p>"<strong>The Foxy Lass</strong>" is an act of <strong>Active Literature</strong>. This is not a "tribute" to James Joyce; it is a direct, word-for-word translation of his prose into a modern, 98 BPM psychedelic funk engine. For the first time, the dense "grammar" of Joyce is made visceral. You can open the book, follow the lines, and let the music act as your guide through the labyrinth.</p>
<p>My mission is simple: to open the world of Joyce to those who may not be able to get there via the printed page alone. By pairing his exact dialogue with a deep, smoky vocal delivery and a low-slung electronic groove, we are piercing the "veil of forgetfulness" that often surrounds these masterworks.</p>

<p>The Vocal: Anchored by a deep, gravelly, and smoky voice delivering lines with the articulate, intimate cadence of a poet.</p>
<p>The Groove: Irresistibly driven by a warm, melodic bass guitar locking perfectly with a tight, crisp drum pattern.</p>

<p>The Atmosphere: Complex, arpeggiated synth leads that build into psychedelic, progressive-rock-like interludes.</p>
<p>The Production: Polished and spacious, using expansive reverb and delay to create a captivating, dream-like breakdown.</p>
