<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Acid Euphoria: Dropping "Cookie Jam (Acid Mix)" 🍭🎹</h2>
<p><!-- /wp:heading --></p>
<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Get ready to lose yourself in the squelch! I am beyond excited to finally announce the release of my latest Acid House project, <strong>"Cookie Jam (Acid Mix),"</strong> officially hitting all platforms on <strong>March 27, 2026</strong>.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>This track isn't just a beat; it’s a journey back to the warehouse roots, reimagined for the modern dance floor. Here is the breakdown of what makes this mix a certified heater:</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} --></p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Sound: Pure Resonant Grit</h3>
<p><!-- /wp:heading --></p>
<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>At its core, "Cookie Jam (Acid Mix)" is a love letter to the <strong>Roland TB-303</strong>. You can expect:</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><!-- wp:list --></p>
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item --></p>
<li><strong>The Signature Squelch:</strong> High-resonance filter sweeps that morph from deep, growling lows to piercing, psychedelic highs.</li>
<p><!-- /wp:list-item --></p>
<p><!-- wp:list-item --></p>
<li><strong>Driving Percussion:</strong> A relentless, punchy 4/4 kick drum paired with crisp, driving hi-hats that keep the energy at a fever pitch throughout the track.</li>
<p><!-- /wp:list-item --></p>
<p><!-- wp:list-item --></p>
<li><strong>Hypnotic Grooves:</strong> A repetitive, trance-inducing bassline designed to lock you into the flow and never let go.</li>
<p><!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<p><!-- /wp:list --></p>
<p><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} --></p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Vibe: Sweet but Gritty</h3>
<p><!-- /wp:heading --></p>
<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>The "Cookie Jam" theme adds a touch of playful irony to the dark, driving nature of Acid House. We’ve sampled vocal chops that ask the age-old question—<em>"Who stole the cookie from the cookie jar?"</em>—twisting them through heavy distortion and delay to create an atmosphere that is equal parts whimsical and underground.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} --></p>

<p><!-- /wp:heading --></p>
<p><!-- wp:list --></p>
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item --></p>
<li><strong>Project Title:</strong> Cookie Jam (Acid Mix)</li>
<p><!-- /wp:list-item --></p>
<p><!-- wp:list-item --></p>
<li><strong>Genre:</strong> Acid House / Techno</li>
<p><!-- /wp:list-item --></p>
<p><!-- wp:list-item --></p>
<li><strong>Official Release Date:</strong> March 27, 2026</li>
<p><!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<p><!-- /wp:list --></p>
<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Mark your calendars and prepare your eardrums. We’re taking it back to the acid rain.</p>
