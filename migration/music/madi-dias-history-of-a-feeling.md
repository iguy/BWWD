<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Madi Dias - History of a Feeling</h1>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I am late to Madi Dias party. First time listening to <strong>History of Feeling </strong> (which looks like it may be Madi Dais's 4th album). </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There is no messing around with words in any of these lyrics. To-the-point and precise. And beyond, being clear - the instructions are how we all wished we had handled that situation.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the first listen the tracks all sound fresh and familiar - not repeated or sounding like anyone else, just a sense of comfort. I keep thinking of Karen Carpenter or Tracy Chapman and how easy they are to understand. Madi Dias shares that stage - in that I can understand all the lyrics (without having to look them up). All the tracks have a rich storytelling vibe. <br></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Think of Me</strong>, and <strong>Crying in Public </strong>are stand out tracks on tracks. <strong>Forever </strong>reminds me of <strong>Jenny Wilson. </strong></p>



<p align="center"><iframe loading="lazy" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/6ouu4XNaN3pQMRo6BUvsaV?utm_source=generator" width="100%" height="380" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture"></iframe></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The MUNA Remix will be on heavy rotation. </p>
