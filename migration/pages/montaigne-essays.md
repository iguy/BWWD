</div></div></div></div><div class="et_pb_row_1 et_pb_row et_block_row"><div class="et_pb_column_1 et_pb_column et_pb_column_1_2 et_block_column et_pb_css_mix_blend_mode_passthrough"><div class="et_pb_text_2 et_pb_text et_pb_bg_layout_light et_pb_module et_block_module"><div class="et_pb_text_inner"><h3>Why Montaigne?</h3>
<p>Michel de Montaigne's essays, written in the 16th century, continue to resonate with surprising relevance today. What started as a "get the most for my money" Audible purchase evolved into a deep appreciation for his timeless wisdom.</p>
<p>After years of having the audiobook sit untouched, I finally gave Montaigne the attention he deserved. But I quickly realized that audio alone wasn't enough to truly absorb his complex ideas.</p>
<p><!-- notionvc: fd4b5b02-fc51-4850-afd1-a0dd69e132c7 --></p>
</div></div></div><div class="et_pb_column_2 et_pb_column et_pb_column_1_2 et-last-child et_block_column et_pb_css_mix_blend_mode_passthrough"><div class="et_pb_text_3 et_pb_text et_pb_bg_layout_light et_pb_module et_block_module"><div class="et_pb_text_inner"><h3>My Approach</h3>
<p>For each essay, I follow a consistent framework of questions, yet the answers are uniquely different every time. This structure helps me unpack Montaigne's thoughts while adding my personal reflections.</p>
<p>At the end of each analysis, I ask: "What am I missing? How does this apply to our modern world?" This bridges the 16th century to our contemporary challenges in technology, relationships, education, and communication.</p>
