Achilles family tree

Achilles is half god, half mortal: his mother is the sea nymph Thetis and his father is King Peleus, a grandson of Zeus. Parents sit above their children; a thick line joins a couple.

Gold: gods and nymphs. Black: the two heroes. Red curve: Achilles and Patroclus. Ancient sources don't always agree; this follows the most common versions.

Achilles's family at a glance

  • Mother: Thetis, a sea nymph, daughter of Nereus and Doris
  • Father: Peleus, mortal king of Phthia
  • Grandparents: Aeacus and Endeïs (father's side); Nereus and Doris (mother's side)
  • Great-grandfather: Zeus, through Aeacus
  • Uncle and cousin: Telamon, and his son Ajax the Great, who also fights at Troy
  • Wife and son: Deidameia, princess of Scyros, and their son Pyrrhus (Neoptolemus)

Patroclus's family tree

  • Father: Menoetius, king of Opus
  • Grandparents: Actor and Aegina, in one ancient version; his mother's name varies between sources
  • Link to Achilles: Aegina is the mother of both Menoetius and Aeacus, so in this version Patroclus is Peleus's cousin and Achilles's first cousin once removed. Miller doesn't use the connection; in the novel Patroclus arrives at Phthia as an exiled stranger
  • Foster father: Peleus, who takes him in after his exile
